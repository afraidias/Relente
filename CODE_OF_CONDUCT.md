# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@afraidias](https://github.com/afraidias)) may edit or remove comments, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may block people from the repository.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports will be handled confidentially.
